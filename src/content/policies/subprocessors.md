This is the list of subprocessors Unkeyed, Inc. uses to process customer personal data for Unkey. It is the authoritative list for the [Data Processing Agreement](https://unkey.com/policies/dpa).

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Unkeyed gives 30 days' notice before a new or replacement subprocessor processes customer personal data. Notice is given by updating this page and by email to customers who subscribe to updates. To subscribe, email [privacy@unkey.com](mailto:privacy@unkey.com).
