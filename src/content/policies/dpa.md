This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is between Unkeyed, Inc. ("Unkeyed") and the customer that accepts it under Section 14 ("Customer"). Unkeyed provides the Unkey product at [https://unkey.com](https://unkey.com) (the "Service").

This DPA supplements the Terms of Use at [https://unkey.com/policies/terms](https://unkey.com/policies/terms) and any order, subscription, or other written terms the parties agree for the Service (together, the "Agreement"). Unkeyed, Inc. is the contracting party and the processor under this DPA. Words defined in the Agreement keep those meanings. If this DPA uses a word that the Agreement does not define, and Privacy Laws define it, the Privacy Laws meaning applies.

If these documents conflict, this order controls: (1) the Standard Contractual Clauses, where they apply and the conflict concerns a transfer they cover; (2) this DPA; (3) the Agreement; (4) the Privacy Policy at [https://unkey.com/policies/privacy](https://unkey.com/policies/privacy). Liability under this DPA follows the limits in the Agreement, except where Privacy Laws or the Standard Contractual Clauses do not allow that limit. **PLACEHOLDER:** any separate liability cap for data-protection claims.

## 1. Definitions

**Account Data** means personal data about Customer's relationship with Unkeyed, such as the name and email address of people Customer allows to administer the account, and billing contacts. The Privacy Policy describes account information collected at registration, including email address.

**Customer Personal Data** means personal data that Customer, or a person acting for Customer, submits to the Service, and personal data Unkeyed processes on Customer's documented instructions in order to provide the Service. It includes personal data of Customer's end users when that data passes through the Service. It does not include Account Data, except where the same information is also stored in the Service as customer content.

**Personal data breach** means a breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data.

**Privacy Laws** means the laws that apply to the processing this DPA covers. They include the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"); the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018; the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection; and US state privacy laws that apply to the processing, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA"). "Controller", "processor", "business", "service provider", "personal data", "personal information", "processing", "sell", "share", and "supervisory authority" have the meanings those laws give them.

**Standard Contractual Clauses** or **SCCs** means the clauses annexed to European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021 ("EU SCCs"), and, for transfers from the United Kingdom, those clauses as modified by the International Data Transfer Addendum issued by the UK Information Commissioner under section 119A of the Data Protection Act 2018 and laid before Parliament on 2 February 2022 ("UK Addendum"). Both are incorporated into this DPA by reference. Transfers under this DPA are made on that basis. Unkeyed does not participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, or the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and those frameworks are not a transfer tool under this DPA.

**Subprocessor** means a third party Unkeyed engages to process Customer Personal Data in order to provide the Service. The current subprocessors are listed in Annex B. A later subprocessor is authorized only under Section 6.

## 2. Roles and instructions

For Customer Personal Data, Customer is the controller and Unkeyed is the processor. Where Customer processes that data as a processor for someone else, Unkeyed is Customer's subprocessor.

Unkeyed processes Customer Personal Data only to provide the Service described in the Agreement and Annex A, and only on Customer's documented instructions, including the instructions in this DPA about international transfers. If a law that applies to Unkeyed requires processing beyond those instructions, Unkeyed will tell Customer of that requirement before processing, unless the law forbids the notice on important grounds of public interest.

Customer instructs Unkeyed to process Customer Personal Data as needed to provide the Service. That instruction includes replication of data between the regions where Unkey's API runs. The API runs in more than one region. The primary region is the United States.

Unkeyed will tell Customer if it reasonably believes an instruction violates Privacy Laws, and it may pause that instruction until Customer confirms or changes it.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

Customer is responsible for having a lawful ground for the processing, for the accuracy of Customer Personal Data, and for the way Customer collected it. Customer's instructions must not put Unkeyed in breach of Privacy Laws. Customer will give its users and end users any notice that Privacy Laws require.

**PLACEHOLDER:** whether Customer must indemnify Unkeyed for claims arising from unlawful Customer Personal Data or from an unlawful instruction.

## 4. Limits on use of Customer Personal Data

Unkeyed will not sell or share Customer Personal Data, in the sense those words have under the CCPA. Unkeyed will not retain, use, or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship with Customer, except as this DPA, the Agreement, or Privacy Laws permit.

Unkeyed will not combine Customer Personal Data with personal data it received from another customer, except where that is required to provide the Service or where Customer instructs it. **PLACEHOLDER:** the technical description of how one customer's data is separated from another's.

To the extent the CCPA requires it, Unkeyed understands these restrictions and will comply with them as a service provider.

## 5. Records and audits

Unkeyed will keep records sufficient to show that it is meeting this DPA. On a written request made at a reasonable interval, Unkeyed will provide information Customer reasonably needs for that purpose and cannot get on its own.

Unkeyed is SOC 2 Type II certified. A customer can request the report through the [Trust Center](https://trust.inc/unkey). Unkeyed may answer an audit request by providing that report and other documents that bear on the Customer Personal Data.

## 7. Confidentiality and security

Unkeyed will ensure that anyone it authorizes to process Customer Personal Data is under a duty of confidentiality.

Unkeyed will keep technical and organizational measures that provide a level of security appropriate to the risk, taking into account the state of the art, the cost of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of the processing. Annex C describes the measures Unkeyed can state today. Items that are not yet documented are marked there.

Unkeyed may disclose Customer Personal Data to professional advisers and auditors who need it to carry out this DPA or the Agreement and who owe Unkeyed a duty of confidentiality.

## 8. Personal data breaches

If Unkeyed becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, Unkeyed will notify Customer without undue delay and, in any event, within **PLACEHOLDER: breach notification window** after Unkeyed becomes aware of it.

The notice will include, so far as Unkeyed knows it at the time: the nature of the breach, the categories of data and the approximate numbers of people and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures Unkeyed has taken or proposes. Unkeyed will supplement the notice as it learns more.

Unkeyed will take reasonable steps that are within its control to contain and remedy the breach. It will also provide the cooperation Customer reasonably needs, and that Unkeyed can provide from the information it holds, so Customer can notify a supervisory authority and affected people when Privacy Laws require Customer to do so.

A notice under this section is not an admission of fault or liability.

**PLACEHOLDER:** whether these duties are narrower when the breach results from Customer's acts or omissions.

## 9. International transfers

The primary region for the Service is the United States. Unkey's API runs in more than one region and replicates data between those regions. Customer instructs that replication. Providing the Service therefore involves transferring Customer Personal Data to the United States and between the regions where the API runs.

If Unkeyed transfers Customer Personal Data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, it will use the safeguards in this Section 9.

If Unkeyed is legally required to disclose, it will give Customer notice and reasonable cooperation so Customer can seek a protective order, unless the law prohibits notice. Unkeyed will not volunteer Customer Personal Data to a public authority.

## 10. Impact assessments

Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information Unkeyed has, Unkeyed will give Customer reasonable help with a data protection impact assessment, and with prior consultation of a supervisory authority, where Privacy Laws require Customer to carry that out and Customer cannot complete it without Unkeyed. Each party stays responsible for the part of an assessment that Privacy Laws assign to it.

## 11. Requests from data subjects

If a person asks Unkeyed to exercise a privacy right in Customer Personal Data, Unkeyed will direct that person to Customer and will tell Customer about the request, unless the law prohibits the notice. Customer is responsible for deciding and sending the response.

Where Customer cannot respond using the Service alone, Unkeyed will provide reasonable technical help, given the nature of the processing. That help may include exporting or deleting the relevant Customer Personal Data that Unkeyed holds.

**PLACEHOLDER:** whether help with these requests is included in the fees or charged separately, and at what rate.

Customer is responsible for the lawful basis of the processing, including any consent it relies on, and for keeping the records of that basis.

## 12. Return and deletion

When the Agreement ends, Unkeyed will, at Customer's written choice, return Customer Personal Data to Customer or delete it. Unkeyed may retain Customer Personal Data where a law requires the retention. In that case Unkeyed will isolate it from ordinary processing and delete it when the legal duty ends.

**PLACEHOLDER:** how long Customer has after the Agreement ends to choose return or deletion, and what Unkeyed does if Customer does not choose.

On Customer's request, Unkeyed will confirm deletion in writing. Where the EU SCCs apply, that confirmation is the deletion certification those clauses describe, and Unkeyed provides it when Customer asks.

## 13. When Unkeyed is a controller

The parties are not joint controllers.

Unkeyed is an independent controller of Account Data. It processes Account Data to run the customer relationship, to bill for the Service, to keep accounting and tax records, to secure accounts, to detect abuse of the Service, and to meet legal duties that apply to Unkeyed. The Privacy Policy describes that processing.

**PLACEHOLDER:** whether Unkeyed is also an independent controller of any service telemetry, and which fields that covers. Until that choice is made, telemetry that identifies an end user of Customer, and the contents of API requests Customer sends through the Service, are Customer Personal Data that Unkeyed processes as a processor.

Processing that Unkeyed does as a controller follows the Privacy Policy. It does not reduce Unkeyed's processor duties for Customer Personal Data.
