This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is between Unkeyed, Inc. ("Unkeyed") and the customer that uses Unkey ("Customer"). It takes effect on 27 October 2026. Unkeyed provides the Unkey product at [https://unkey.com](https://unkey.com) (the "Service").

This DPA is part of the Terms of Use at [https://unkey.com/policies/terms](https://unkey.com/policies/terms) and of any order, subscription, or other written terms the parties agree for the Service (together, the "Agreement"). Unkeyed, Inc. is the contracting party and the processor under this DPA. Words defined in the Agreement keep those meanings. If this DPA uses a word that the Agreement does not define, and Privacy Laws define it, the Privacy Laws meaning applies.

If these documents conflict, this order controls: (1) the Standard Contractual Clauses, where they apply and the conflict concerns a transfer they cover; (2) this DPA; (3) the Agreement; (4) the Privacy Policy at [https://unkey.com/policies/privacy](https://unkey.com/policies/privacy). There is no separate liability cap for data-protection claims. Liability under this DPA follows the limits in the Terms of Use, except where Privacy Laws or the Standard Contractual Clauses do not allow that limit.

## 1. Definitions

**Account Data** means personal data about Customer's relationship with Unkeyed, such as the name and email address of people Customer allows to administer the account. The Privacy Policy describes account information collected at registration, including email address.

**Aggregated Telemetry** means service and usage telemetry that Unkeyed has aggregated for service operation, billing, security, and improvement metrics.

**Customer Personal Data** means personal data that Customer, or a person acting for Customer, submits to the Service, and personal data Unkeyed processes on Customer's documented instructions in order to provide the Service. It includes personal data of Customer's end users when that data passes through the Service, and logs from compute workloads. It does not include Account Data or Aggregated Telemetry, except where the same information is also stored in the Service as customer content.

**Personal data breach** means a breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data.

**Privacy Laws** means the laws that apply to the processing this DPA covers. They include the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"); the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018; the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection; and US state privacy laws that apply to the processing, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA"). "Controller", "processor", "business", "service provider", "personal data", "personal information", "processing", "sell", "share", and "supervisory authority" have the meanings those laws give them.

**Standard Contractual Clauses** or **SCCs** means the clauses annexed to European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021 ("EU SCCs"), and, for transfers from the United Kingdom, those clauses as modified by the International Data Transfer Addendum issued by the UK Information Commissioner under section 119A of the Data Protection Act 2018 and laid before Parliament on 2 February 2022 ("UK Addendum"). Both are incorporated into this DPA by reference. Transfers under this DPA are made on that basis. Unkeyed does not participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, or the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and those frameworks are not a transfer tool under this DPA.

**Subprocessor** means a third party Unkeyed engages to process Customer Personal Data in order to provide the Service. The current subprocessors are listed on the [Subprocessors page](https://unkey.com/policies/subprocessors), which is the authoritative list and is incorporated into Annex B. A later subprocessor is authorized only under Section 6.

## 2. Roles and instructions

For Customer Personal Data, Customer is the controller and Unkeyed is the processor. Where Customer processes that data as a processor for someone else, Unkeyed is Customer's subprocessor.

Unkeyed processes Customer Personal Data only to provide the Service described in the Agreement and Annex A, and only on Customer's documented instructions, including the instructions in this DPA about international transfers. If a law that applies to Unkeyed requires processing beyond those instructions, Unkeyed will tell Customer of that requirement before processing, unless the law forbids the notice on important grounds of public interest.

Customer instructs Unkeyed to process Customer Personal Data as needed to provide the Service. The API and compute run on AWS. The primary region is the United States. AWS and PlanetScale keep read-only replicas in the regions described on the [Subprocessors page](https://unkey.com/policies/subprocessors).

Unkeyed will tell Customer if it reasonably believes an instruction violates Privacy Laws, and it may pause that instruction until Customer confirms or changes it.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

Customer is responsible for having a lawful ground for the processing, for the accuracy of Customer Personal Data, and for the way Customer collected it. Customer's instructions must not put Unkeyed in breach of Privacy Laws. Customer will give its users and end users any notice that Privacy Laws require.

Customer will indemnify Unkeyed against claims and losses arising from unlawful Customer Personal Data or from an unlawful instruction.

Customer must not submit special-category data, sensitive data under US state privacy laws, or government identifiers. That prohibition does not cover a key or rate-limit identifier Customer chooses, or logs Customer sends through Unkey's compute product.

## 4. Limits on use of Customer Personal Data

Unkeyed will not sell or share Customer Personal Data, in the sense those words have under the CCPA. Unkeyed will not retain, use, or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship with Customer, except as this DPA, the Agreement, or Privacy Laws permit.

Unkeyed will not combine Customer Personal Data with personal data it received from another customer, except where that is required to provide the Service or where Customer instructs it. Customer Personal Data is logically separated by workspace identifier.

To the extent the CCPA requires it, Unkeyed understands these restrictions and will comply with them as a service provider.

## 5. Records and audits

Unkeyed will keep records sufficient to show that it is meeting this DPA.

Unkeyed is SOC 2 Type II certified. On a written request, Unkeyed will first provide that report, which a customer can request through the [Trust Center](https://trust.inc/unkey), and will answer reasonable security questionnaires.

A further audit is available only if a regulator requires it, or after a personal data breach. It may occur at most once a year. Customer must give 30 days' written notice. The audit is under confidentiality, at Customer's cost, and is conducted remotely or as a documentary review. Unkeyed has no offices or data centers of its own.

Where the EU SCCs apply, the audit rights in Clause 8.9 are exercised only through this Section 5.

## 6. Subprocessors

Customer gives Unkeyed general authorization to use the subprocessors named on the [Subprocessors page](https://unkey.com/policies/subprocessors), and to add or replace subprocessors, to process Customer Personal Data for the purposes listed there. Unkeyed will bind each subprocessor, by written terms, to protect Customer Personal Data to a standard no lower than this DPA. If a subprocessor fails to meet those terms, Unkeyed remains responsible to Customer for that failure.

Unkeyed will give 30 days' notice before a new or replacement subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data. Notice is given by updating the [Subprocessors page](https://unkey.com/policies/subprocessors) and by email to customers who subscribe to those updates. A customer subscribes by emailing privacy@unkey.com.

Customer may object within 14 days of that notice, on reasonable grounds relating to data protection. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, Customer may terminate the affected part of the Service and receive a prorated refund of prepaid fees for that part.

Where the EU SCCs apply, Clause 9 uses Option 2 (general written authorisation). The minimum time for prior notice of a subprocessor change is 30 days, as this Section 6 states. The [Subprocessors page](https://unkey.com/policies/subprocessors) is the agreed list.

## 7. Confidentiality and security

Unkeyed will ensure that anyone it authorizes to process Customer Personal Data is under a duty of confidentiality. Staff who can access Customer Personal Data sign confidentiality agreements, take security training yearly, and are subject to background checks where lawful.

Unkeyed will keep technical and organizational measures that provide a level of security appropriate to the risk, taking into account the state of the art, the cost of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of the processing. Annex C describes those measures.

Unkeyed may disclose Customer Personal Data to professional advisers and auditors who need it to carry out this DPA or the Agreement and who owe Unkeyed a duty of confidentiality.

## 8. Personal data breaches

If Unkeyed confirms a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, Unkeyed will notify Customer without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after that confirmation.

The notice will include, so far as Unkeyed knows it at the time: the nature of the breach, the categories of data and the approximate numbers of people and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures Unkeyed has taken or proposes. Unkeyed will supplement the notice as it learns more.

Unkeyed will take reasonable steps that are within its control to contain and remedy the breach. It will also provide the cooperation Customer reasonably needs, and that Unkeyed can provide from the information it holds, so Customer can notify a supervisory authority and affected people when Privacy Laws require Customer to do so.

Where Customer caused the personal data breach, Unkeyed's duties under this section are limited to notifying Customer and providing the information Unkeyed has about the breach.

A notice under this section is not an admission of fault or liability.

## 9. International transfers

The primary region for the Service is the United States. AWS and PlanetScale keep read-only replicas in the regions named on the [Subprocessors page](https://unkey.com/policies/subprocessors). Customer instructs that processing. Providing the Service therefore involves transferring Customer Personal Data to the United States and keeping read-only copies in those replica regions.

If Unkeyed transfers Customer Personal Data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, it will use the safeguards in this Section 9.

### 9.1 Transfers from the EEA

The EU SCCs are incorporated by reference and completed as follows.

- Module One (controller to controller) applies to Account Data and Aggregated Telemetry, which Section 13 treats as data Unkeyed processes as a controller.
- Module Two (controller to processor) applies when Customer is a controller and Unkeyed is a processor.
- Module Three (processor to processor) applies when Customer is a processor and Unkeyed is a subprocessor.
- The optional docking clause in Clause 7 applies.
- Clause 9 uses Option 2, as Section 6 describes, with 30 days' prior notice of subprocessor changes.
- The optional language in Clause 11 does not apply.
- Under Clause 17, the EU SCCs are governed by the law of Ireland. Under Clause 18, disputes are resolved in the courts of Ireland.
- Annex I of the EU SCCs is completed by Annex B of this DPA. Annex II is completed by Annex C. Annex III, the subprocessor list, is the [Subprocessors page](https://unkey.com/policies/subprocessors), which Annex B incorporates.
- The parties are treated as having signed the EU SCCs, including the annexes, when Customer accepts this DPA under Section 14.

### 9.2 Transfers from the United Kingdom

Ex-UK transfers are made under the UK Addendum, which is incorporated by reference and completed by Annex D. The mandatory clauses of the approved UK Addendum are part of this DPA. Those clauses are the standard UK modifications to the EU SCCs.

### 9.3 Transfers from Switzerland

For personal data subject to the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, the EU SCCs apply with the standard Swiss modifications: references to the GDPR include that Act; the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner is the competent authority for those Swiss transfers, alongside the competent EU authority for GDPR transfers; and references to an EU Member State do not stop a person in Switzerland from enforcing rights in their place of habitual residence.

### 9.4 Government access requests

If a public authority demands Customer Personal Data from Unkeyed, Unkeyed will ask the authority to seek the data from Customer, and Unkeyed may provide Customer's basic contact details for that purpose. If Unkeyed is legally required to disclose, it will give Customer notice and reasonable cooperation so Customer can seek a protective order, unless the law prohibits notice. Unkeyed will not volunteer Customer Personal Data to a public authority.

If a transfer tool in this Section 9 ceases to be valid, or a supervisory authority orders a transfer to stop, the parties will suspend the affected transfer and work to put a lawful alternative in place.

This DPA is governed by the same terms as Section 8 of the Terms of Use. Disputes are resolved under that section, including its arbitration agreement. The Federal Arbitration Act governs the interpretation and enforcement of the arbitration agreement. Unless the parties agree otherwise, or the batch-arbitration process in the Terms applies, arbitration is conducted in the county where Customer resides. The Terms of Use do not name a separate state or country as the governing law of the contract. Clause 17 and Clause 18 of the EU SCCs still govern the transfers those clauses cover.

## 10. Impact assessments

Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information Unkeyed has, Unkeyed will give Customer reasonable help with a data protection impact assessment, and with prior consultation of a supervisory authority, where Privacy Laws require Customer to carry that out and Customer cannot complete it without Unkeyed. Each party stays responsible for the part of an assessment that Privacy Laws assign to it.

## 11. Requests from data subjects

If a person asks Unkeyed to exercise a privacy right in Customer Personal Data, Unkeyed will direct that person to Customer and will tell Customer about the request, unless the law prohibits the notice. Customer is responsible for deciding and sending the response.

Where Customer cannot respond using the Service alone, Unkeyed will provide reasonable technical help, given the nature of the processing. That help may include exporting or deleting the relevant Customer Personal Data that Unkeyed holds. That help is free within reason. Unkeyed may charge a reasonable fee for excessive or repetitive requests.

Customer is responsible for the lawful basis of the processing, including any consent it relies on, and for keeping the records of that basis.

## 12. Return and deletion

When the Agreement ends, Customer has 30 days to export Customer Personal Data. Unkeyed will delete Customer Personal Data within 90 days after that export period ends. Unkeyed may retain Customer Personal Data where a law requires the retention. In that case Unkeyed will isolate it from ordinary processing and delete it when the legal duty ends.

On Customer's request, Unkeyed will confirm deletion in writing. Where the EU SCCs apply, that confirmation is the deletion certification those clauses describe, and Unkeyed provides it when Customer asks.

## 13. When Unkeyed is a controller

The parties are not joint controllers.

Unkeyed is an independent controller of Account Data and of Aggregated Telemetry. It processes Account Data to run the customer relationship, to bill for the Service, to keep accounting and tax records, to secure accounts, to detect abuse of the Service, and to meet legal duties that apply to Unkeyed. It processes Aggregated Telemetry for service operation, billing, security, and improvement metrics. The Privacy Policy describes Unkeyed's own processing.

Identifiers, IP addresses, and request metadata of Customer's end users, and logs from compute workloads, are Customer Personal Data that Unkeyed processes as a processor. They are not Aggregated Telemetry.

Processing that Unkeyed does as a controller follows the Privacy Policy. It does not reduce Unkeyed's processor duties for Customer Personal Data.

## 14. Acceptance

Customer accepts this DPA by using Unkey. This DPA forms part of the Terms of Use. It takes effect on 27 October 2026.

On request, Unkeyed will provide a countersigned copy. James Perkins, CEO, signs for Unkeyed, Inc.

## Annex A. Details of processing

**Subject matter.** Unkeyed processes Customer Personal Data to provide the Unkey Service.

**Duration.** For the term of the Agreement. After the Agreement ends, Customer has 30 days to export, and Unkeyed deletes Customer Personal Data within 90 days after that export period, except where a law requires a longer retention.

**Nature and purpose.** Unkeyed receives, stores, organizes, retrieves, transmits, and deletes Customer Personal Data as needed to provide the Service. It secures that data, including by storing key material encrypted at rest in S3. The API and compute run on AWS. The primary region is the United States. AWS and PlanetScale keep read-only replicas in the regions described on the [Subprocessors page](https://unkey.com/policies/subprocessors). Unkeyed also uses the subprocessors on that page for the purposes listed there. Customer Personal Data is logically separated by workspace identifier.

**Data subjects.**

- Customer's team members.
- Customer's end users.

**Categories of personal data.**

- For Customer's team members: name and email address.
- For Customer's end users: identifiers, IP addresses, and request metadata.
- Logs from compute workloads.

**Special categories.** Customer must not submit special-category data, sensitive data under US state privacy laws, or government identifiers, except as a key or rate-limit identifier Customer chooses, or in logs Customer sends through Unkey's compute product.
