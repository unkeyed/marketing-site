![New region: ap-southeast-1](/images/changelog/2026-07-16/ap-southeast-1.png)

You can now run your deployments in **ap-southeast-1** (Singapore).

If your users are in Southeast Asia, Australia, or India, their requests no longer need to cross an ocean. Deploy to Singapore and Unkey routes each request to the nearest region, cutting round-trip latency significantly for the entire region.

To enable it, open your project's **Settings**, go to **Runtime settings → Regions**, and select `ap-southeast-1`. The new region applies on your next deployment. Production and preview environments have their own region configuration, so you can enable it for one without the other. Read more in the [regions documentation](https://www.unkey.com/docs/build-and-deploy/regions).
