## What you'll do

You'll wear many hats here: alert triage and incident response, planning EKS cluster migrations and upgrades, and contributing to product development. We're a small team, and everyone's contributions are wanted.

On the SRE side, reliability is priority number one. You'll assist product engineers with planning, capacity needs, and safe, secure rollouts. You'll also participate in a follow-the-sun on-call rotation.

This role requires working Americas time zone hours. You can be based anywhere; you do not need to live in the Americas.

## Technologies

- **Cloud:** AWS: EKS, EC2, IAM Identity Center, Route 53; gRPC
- **Kubernetes:** Karpenter, Helm, ArgoCD, gVisor
- **Networking:** Cilium (ClusterMesh, WireGuard), AWS VPC CNI, routing (OSI model)
- **Infrastructure as code:** OpenTofu/Terraform
- **Security:** SIEM, GuardDuty, IAM Access Analyzer
- **Observability:** OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Thanos, Loki, Tempo, Grafana, Hubble, eBPF
- **External platforms:** depot.dev, Restate, PlanetScale, incident.io
- **Languages/Frameworks:** Go (our services), Protobuf (buf.build), Shell, Terraform

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years running production Kubernetes (EKS).
- On-call experience, incident management, incident response, and post-mortems.
- AWS IAM: you can write a least-privilege policy, explain a trust policy, and read CloudTrail to see who did what.
- You understand Kubernetes scheduling well enough to predict how kube-scheduler and Karpenter will treat a given spread constraint, PDB, or priority class.
- Deep networking experience.

## Nice to have

- Cilium in production, especially ClusterMesh or CNI chaining via AWS VPC CNI.
- SOC 2/ISO 27001 experience with control implementation.
- Experience running multi-tenant platforms where customers run their own untrusted code.
- Talos Kubernetes.
